# SKY-001

## What it is

**Skyfall AI** **engineering world model** (roundup: **Sky 001**): a **hierarchical composer** plus **construction world model** that turns a **prompt or product image** into a **part/assembly tree**, **per-part 3D build states**, and **2D sketches between states**; a parallel path turns **building outlines** into **unit and room layouts** (BIM-style). Skyfall positions it as **spatial planning**, not next-token LLM CAD chat. Public materials describe a **research preview** with **waitlist access**, **no released weights**, and **no self-serve API** yet.

## Why keep

Track when choosing **text/image-to-CAD decomposition** vendors or comparing **world-model CAD** claims against local mesh/text-to-3D tools.

## When to reach for it

You get on the **official waitlist** at Skyfall and run a **guided pilot** on mechanical or architectural decomposition benchmarks you already trust. Not a pick for **local open weights** today.

## Caveats

**Closed preview**; roundup and Skyfall blog cite **large speed/cost gaps vs frontier LLMs** on their **internal CAD/BIM benchmarks**, not independent leaderboards. **No local run**; ignore unrelated **“Skyfall”** HF repos. Commercial timing in third-party writeups points to **2027**; verify on Skyfall before planning production.
