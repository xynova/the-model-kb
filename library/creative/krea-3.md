# Krea 3

## What it is

Krea hosted image edit model at native 2K with selective edits and up to six references. Natural-language generate and edit; Krea targets under 8 seconds end to end on their service for most 2K jobs.

## Why keep

Compare when choosing a paid edit API for catalog, campaign, or precision product work where speed and label fidelity matter.

## When to reach for it

Krea app or `POST https://api.krea.ai/generate/image/krea/krea-3` (also on fal, Replicate, OpenRouter, and others) when you need fast iterative edits without re-shooting.

## Caveats

No open weights today; the roundup mentioned possible future open source, but only hosted access exists now. List price about $0.06 per 2K image and $0.03 at 1K on Krea; references and `high` thinking mode cost extra. Third-party hosts may be slower than Krea’s own queue.
