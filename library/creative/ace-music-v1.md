# ACE Music v1

## What it is

**ACE Music v1** (ACE Studio) is a **text-to-song** generator: full vocals or instrumentals from prompts, lyrics, MIDI, or hummed melody, with steering for delivery (e.g. husky voice, soft hum). The product bundles **stem separation** and **MIDI export** for downstream editing. On launch it ranked **#1** on Artificial Analysis **AA-Music-Vocal v1.1** (1160 Elo vs Suno v6 at 1144). It runs only in the **ACE Music web app** today; **no public API** and **no open weights** for the flagship.

## Why keep

Track the **closed vocal leaderboard leader** and compare when choosing hosted song generation; note the promised **ACE Music v1 Flash** open release vs the already-open **ACE-Step** family from the same ecosystem.

## When to reach for it

You want **melody-first song drafting** and studio-style export in a browser, and you accept **paid plans** for downloads and commercial rights (free tier is credit-limited, no downloads per third-party pricing writeups).

## Caveats

**Proprietary** flagship; self-host today means **ACE-Step** (different model), not v1. **ACE Music v1 Flash** is announced for **open source in late 2026** (timing may differ from social posts). Leaderboard margins are small and vote counts move. Roundup name: **Ace Music V1**.
